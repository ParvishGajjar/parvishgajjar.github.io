How I Think

I want the kind of problem that fights back a little.

That usually means ambiguity to untangle, assumptions to test, and a team that needs more than just code. I reach for the pattern, the pressure points, and the smallest clear structure that makes the rest of the work easier to reason about — whether that's an SCD2 pattern, a Python script that replaces a spreadsheet, or a retrieval step that makes a model actually useful.

01

Challenge energizes me

I prefer work that asks for real thought, not repetition. Complexity is interesting when it leads to insight.

02

Structure is how I think

I reach for models, pipelines, and clean abstractions that make a messy dataset easier to hold in one hand.

03

Execution matters

Curiosity is half. The other half is shipping something dependable — that holds up when a 5B-row table runs through it.
