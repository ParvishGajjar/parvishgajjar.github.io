How I Think

I want the kind of problem that fights back a little.

That usually means ambiguity to untangle, assumptions to test, and a team that needs more than just code. I tend to look for the pattern, the pressure points, and the smallest clear structure that makes the rest of the work easier to reason about. In practice, that often means moving between SQL, Python scripting, and quick POC-style exploration until the right shape of the solution becomes obvious.

Challenge energizes me

I enjoy work that requires real thought, not just repetition. Complexity is interesting when it leads to insight.

Structure is how I think

I naturally reach for models, systems, and clean abstractions that make a messy problem easier to understand.

Execution matters

Curiosity is only half of it. I care about quality, collaboration, and turning ideas into something dependable.
